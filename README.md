# Nova-Aetas

The goal of Nova Aetas is to explore a new paradigm for ASIC development in which AI is integrated throughout the entire hardware-design flow—from architectural exploration and RTL generation through verification, debugging, optimization, physical design, and documentation. The project uses an open-source RISC-V-based design and open-source EDA tools to provide a reproducible environment for evaluating both the capabilities and limitations of AI agents performing real hardware-engineering tasks. A key objective is to investigate not only how effectively AI can accelerate design and verification, but also how to identify and mitigate risks introduced by agentic development, including incorrect or insecure RTL, prompt injection, IP leakage, benchmark contamination, and other forms of AI misuse.
